Introduction

At Real Deal, we care about our customers and respect their privacy. We want you to feel comfortable and safe when browsing our website, checking out our graphic tees, or placing an order. This Privacy Policy explains the information we collect, how we use it, and how we keep it protected.

Information We Collect

When you shop with us or get in touch, we may ask for basic information such as your name, email address, contact number, and delivery address. We may also collect details about your orders so we can provide you with the products you want.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to process your orders, arrange deliveries, answer your questions, and keep you updated about your purchases. It may also help us improve our website and make your shopping experience with Real Deal easier and more enjoyable.

Sharing Your Information

Your privacy matters to us, and we do not sell your personal information. However, we may share the necessary details with trusted service providers, such as a delivery or payment service, when needed to complete your order or operate our store.

Cookies and Website Usage

Our website may use cookies to help it work properly and understand how visitors use our pages. This helps us improve the website and make browsing or shopping for clothing collections more convenient. We will provide further information about any cookies or tracking tools we actually use.

Keeping Your Information Safe

We do our best to protect your personal information and prevent it from being accessed or used without permission. You may also have the right to ask about the information we hold about you, request corrections, or request deletion, depending on applicable privacy laws.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

As Real Deal grows, we may need to update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will post the updated version on this page so you can stay informed about how your information is handled.

Contact Us

Have questions or concerns about your privacy? We'd be happy to hear from you. You can reach us through our Contact page. We're here to help make your experience with Real Deal as smooth and comfortable as possible.
